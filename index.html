I take projects from concept development through 3D visualization and client presentation to on-site execution. Skilled in 3ds Max, Corona Renderer, V-Ray, Lumion and D5 Render, and currently expanding into Unreal Engine for real-time visualization and VR.

How I work

My process

  1. 01
    Brief and referencesPlans, drawings and mood references collected and checked.
  2. 02
    3D modelingThe space built in 3ds Max from the supplied plans.
  3. 03
    Materials and lightingSurfaces, daylight and lamps tuned until the atmosphere is right.
  4. 04
    RenderingFinal images in Corona Renderer, Lumion or D5 Render.
  5. 05
    Finishing and deliveryPost-production in Photoshop, then files in the formats you need.
